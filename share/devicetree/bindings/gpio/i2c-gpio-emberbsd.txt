i2c-gpio FDT attachment

The generic compatible "i2c-gpio" consumes sda-gpios and scl-gpios from FDT
GPIO providers. Both pins must support input and open-drain output. The
attachment uses the common i2c_bitbang engine at its standard-mode timing; it
does not implement the optional i2c-gpio delay property or target-specific
bus speeds. It registers an FDT I2C controller and attaches standard I2C
children. Hardware behavior requires separate target verification.
